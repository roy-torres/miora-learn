    Short lessons, no jargon

    What a biomarker is

    A measurable signal in your blood, like glucose or vitamin D, that reflects how a system is working.

    3 min

    Normal versus optimal

    A lab’s normal range flags disease. An optimal range asks how well you could be doing.

    4 min

    Why patterns beat single numbers

    Markers move together. Reading them as triads shows causes, not only symptoms.

    Terms you will hear in your visit

    Open any term for a one-paragraph answer.

    Metabolic Code

    MIORA’s analysis of 70+ biomarkers across five triads, read alongside your symptom report.

    Triad

    A group of related markers that are read together, because one number alone rarely explains how you feel.

    Optimal range

    A narrower target than the standard lab range, set around how well you function rather than whether you have a disease.

    Care plan

    The steps you and your provider agree on, based on your results, history, and goals. You can ask what each step is for and how it will be measured.

    What the evidence supports

    Not every test or treatment has the same research behind it. Here is how we label what you will read about.

    Well established Mixed or limited Emerging or unproven

    Glucose and cholesterol tests

    Well established

    Fasting glucose is a standard test for diabetes, and LDL cholesterol is a standard guide to heart disease risk and treatment.

    • ADA Standards of Care: Classification and Diagnosis of Diabetes, 2023
    • 2018 AHA/ACC Guideline on the Management of Blood Cholesterol

    Treating vitamin D deficiency

    Well established

    Correcting a true deficiency is standard care.

    • Endocrine Society guideline: Evaluation, Treatment, and Prevention of Vitamin D Deficiency, 2011

    Raising vitamin D above the normal range

    Mixed or limited

    In a trial of more than 25,000 adults without cancer or heart disease, daily vitamin D did not lower the rate of cancer or major heart events.

    • Manson et al., VITAL trial, New England Journal of Medicine, 2019

    Inflammation markers such as hs‑CRP

    Mixed or limited

    A 2003 AHA and CDC statement treated hs-CRP as a marker of heart risk, most useful for people at intermediate risk. It did not recommend testing everyone.

    • Pearson et al., AHA/CDC scientific statement, Circulation, 2003

    Hormone therapy when levels are in the normal range

    Mixed or limited

    For men, the Endocrine Society recommends testosterone therapy only when symptoms and consistently low levels both appear, and advises against routine screening.

    • Bhasin et al., Endocrine Society guideline: Testosterone Therapy in Men With Hypogonadism, 2018

    Scoring 70+ markers as one pattern

    Emerging or unproven

    The Metabolic Code is MIORA’s own framework. We found no independent, peer-reviewed validation of it. Ask MIORA for any published evidence.

    • MIORA, How MIORA works (company description)

    Sources checked November 2026. Guidelines are updated, so ask your provider for the current version. This page is educational and is not medical advice.

    Questions to ask before you book

    • What will this cost today, and over a year?
    • Which of these tests will change my care?
    • What does the research say about each treatment you suggest?
    • How will we know if it is working, and when do we stop?
    • Will my primary doctor see my results?
    • Which of these steps can I do without paying for a program?
