# Project rules

## Design references
1. Always refer to the book `Designing_User_Interfaces_EN_2024_FINAL.pdf` when crafting a UI or layout design.
   - 4px base unit, with presets: 8, 16, 20, 24, etc.
   - Button heights: 48px.
2. When designing for AI, always refer to `_OceanofPDF.com_UX_for_AI_-_Greg_Nudelman.pdf`.
3. All designs must follow the WCAG guidelines: https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/

Both PDFs are in `~/Desktop/` (copies of the UI book also in `~/.openclaw/workspace/references/`).

## Workflow
4. Think through the problem. Read the codebase and write a plan in `tasks/todo.md`.
5. The plan is a checklist of todo items.
6. Check in with me before starting work. I will verify the plan.
7. Then complete the todos one by one, marking them off as you go.
8. At every step, give a high-level explanation of what you changed.
9. Keep every change simple and minimal. Avoid big rewrites.
10. At the end, add a review section in `tasks/todo.md` summarizing the changes.
